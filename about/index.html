Han Xiao, Ph.D.

LinkedIn·X/Twitter·Github·Scholar

I’m VP of AI at Elastic. I started Jina AI in 2020 and ran it as CEO until Elastic (NYSE: ESTC) acquired us in October 2025. Before that I worked on search at Tencent and Zalando. I created Fashion-MNIST, now a standard computer vision benchmark with 13,000+ citations, and did my Ph.D. at TU Munich on adversarial and robust non-parametric Bayesian learning.

I’ve lived and worked across the SF Bay Area, Berlin, Munich, Taipei, Beijing, and Shenzhen. These days I’m in Mountain View.

Go West, young man!
